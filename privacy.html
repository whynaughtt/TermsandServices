ProvinceDex Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Information We Collect

ProvinceDex may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, and game-related information such as collectibles, inventory, and statistics.

We use this information to operate the bot, save game progress, provide features, and prevent abuse.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share information except when necessary to operate the service or comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Retention

Information may be retained while needed to operate ProvinceDex. You may contact the development team to request assistance with your data.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored information, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time.

7. Contact

Contact the ProvinceDex development team through our Discord server for privacy questions or data requests.
